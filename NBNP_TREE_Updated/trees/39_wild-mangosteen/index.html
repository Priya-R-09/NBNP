Wild Mangosteen

முருகல்

Garcinia indica (Thouars) Choisy

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameGarcinia indica (Thouars) Choisy
FamilyClusiaceae.
Common NameWild Mangosteen
Tamil Nameமுருகல்
01Flowering SeasonNovember to February
02Fruiting SeasonApril to June
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • Can help regulate blood sugar levels and improve glucose metabolism.
  • Helps with acidity, indigestion, constipation, and flatulence.
  • Used to make refreshing drinks like kokum sherbet or juice, especially in hot weather.
  • Used in traditional medicine for skin issues like sores and dermatitis, ear infections, and to relieve sunburn.
