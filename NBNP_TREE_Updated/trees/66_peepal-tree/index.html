Peepal tree

அரசமரம்

Ficus religiosa Forssk.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameFicus religiosa Forssk.
FamilyMoraceae
Common NamePeepal tree
Tamil Nameஅரசமரம்
01Flowering SeasonNovember and February
02Fruiting SeasonMarch to May
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • The tree is a great oxygen producer, often planted in sacred spaces.
  • Dried fruits or bark ground into powder and mixed with water or honey.
  • The Bark infusions can strengthen gums and help with toothaches.
  • Latex applied to wounds to promote healing; bark decoctions used for eczema, itching, and ulcers.
