Sandpaper Tree

பராய்

Streblus asper Lour.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameStreblus asper Lour.
FamilyMoraceae
Common NameSandpaper Tree
Tamil Nameபராய்
01Flowering SeasonJanuary to March and again from July to September
02Fruiting SeasonApril to June.
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • Bark decoctions treat fever, diarrhea, and dysentery.
  • Root applications for snakebites, and liver support for jaundice.
  • Animal feed.
  • Bio-insecticide.
  • Fuel and papermaking.
