Botanical NameTerminalia elliptica Willd.

01Flowering SeasonApril to August

02

English Information

  • Bark is used for ulcers, hemorrhages, bronchitis, and diarrhea
  • The plant exhibits anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and antioxidant properties.
