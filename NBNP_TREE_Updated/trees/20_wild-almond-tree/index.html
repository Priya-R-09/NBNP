Wild Almond Tree

பீநாறி

Sterculia foetida L.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameSterculia foetida L.
FamilyMalvaceae
Common NameWild Almond Tree
Tamil Nameபீநாறி
01Flowering SeasonFebruary to May
02Fruiting SeasonMarch to May
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • The seeds are a good source of protein and oil, and are roasted and eaten in some regions.
  • The wood is soft but durable for interior work, and is used to make items like dugout canoes, guitars, and carved toys.
  • Powdered seeds are used as a red pigment.
