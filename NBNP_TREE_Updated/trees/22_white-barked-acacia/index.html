White-Barked Acacia

வெள்வேல்

Acacia leucophloea (Roxb.) Willd.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameAcacia leucophloea (Roxb.) Willd.
FamilyFabaceae
Common NameWhite-Barked Acacia
Tamil Nameவெள்வேல்
01Flowering SeasonAugust to October
02Fruiting SeasonApril to June
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • Used to treat dry coughs, diarrhea, vomiting bronchitis, and bronchial inflammation.
  • Employed to treat skin ailments like leprosy and leukoderma .
  • Employed to treat snake bites, ulcers, and bleeding gums.menstrual problems.
