Bedda nut tree

தான்றிக்காய்

Terminalia bellirica (Gaertn.) Roxb.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameTerminalia bellirica (Gaertn.) Roxb.
FamilyCombretaceae
Common NameBedda nut tree
Tamil Nameதான்றிக்காய்
01Flowering SeasonFebruary to April.
02Fruiting SeasonNovember to February or December to March
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • The fruit is used to treat cough, common cold, asthma, and hoarseness of voice..
  • It is used to help with a variety of digestive issues, including indigestion, flatulence, bloating, and loss of appetite.
  • In traditional medicine, the fruit is used for various eye disorders, including conjunctivitis, corneal opacity, and myopia.
