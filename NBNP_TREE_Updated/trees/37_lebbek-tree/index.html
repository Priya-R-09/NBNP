Lebbek tree

வாகை மரம்

Albizia lebbeck (L.) Benth.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameAlbizia lebbeck (L.) Benth.
FamilyMimosaceae
Common NameLebbek tree
Tamil Nameவாகை மரம்
01Flowering SeasonApril to June or September.
02Fruiting SeasonSeptember–October
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • Albizia lebbeck is used in traditional medicine for a variety of purposes, including treating respiratory issues like asthma and coughs, skin diseases, allergic reactions, and snakebites.
  • The tree is a source of timber in India and Pakistan.
03
