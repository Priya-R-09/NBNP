Tulip-Wood Tree

நெய்க்கொட்டை

Harpullia arborea (Blanco) Radlk.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameHarpullia arborea (Blanco) Radlk.
Familysapindaceae
Common NameTulip-Wood Tree
Tamil Nameநெய்க்கொட்டை
01Flowering SeasonOctober to December
02Fruiting SeasonJune to July.
03Medicinal ValueWhole parts
02

English Information

  • The wood is used to make furniture, construction materials, and other household items.
  • Used in some traditional practices for washing hair.
  • The bark can be used as a fish poison.
  • The seeds and oil from the seeds may be used to treat rheumatism, and a watery exudate from the bark is used to allay pain.
