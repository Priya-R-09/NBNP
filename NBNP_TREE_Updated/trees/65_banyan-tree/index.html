Banyan Tree

ஆலமரம்

Ficus benghalensis L.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameFicus benghalensis L.
FamilyMoraceae
Common NameBanyan Tree
Tamil Nameஆலமரம்
01Flowering SeasonMarch to April.
02Fruiting SeasonMarch to May
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • Wood is used for furniture, door panels, and paper pulp.
  • The tree is a high-oxygen generating tree that filters air contaminants.
  • Aerial roots are used to clean teeth, strengthen gums and prevent bad breath or gum bleeding.
  • The milky latex is applied to wounds, sores, skin infections, and bruises to speed up healing.
