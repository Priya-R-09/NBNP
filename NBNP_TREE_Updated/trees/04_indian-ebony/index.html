Indian Ebony

கருங்காலி

Diospyros ebenum J.Koenig

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameDiospyros ebenum J.Koenig
FamilyEbenaceae
Common NameIndian Ebony
Tamil Nameகருங்காலி
01Flowering SeasonApril and May
02Fruiting SeasonSeptember to November
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • The bark is used for diarrhea, dyspepsia, cough, and asthma
  • Root is used for treating infections with fever.
  • Dried flowers are used to treat urinary and skin infections.
