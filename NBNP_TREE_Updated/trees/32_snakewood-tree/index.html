snakewood tree

எட்டி மரம்

Strychnos nux-vomica L.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY

FamilyLoganiaceae
Common Namesnakewood tree
Tamil Nameஎட்டி மரம்
01Flowering SeasonMarch to May.
02Fruiting SeasonNovember to February
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • Devil's tree is used in traditional medicine to treat a wide range of ailments, including fever, skin diseases, and stomach complaints.
  • The bark is used for issues like malaria, diarrhea, and rheumatism, while the latex from the bark is applied to skin problems.
  • The flowers and roots also have medicinal applications, such as for asthma and leprosy, respectively.
