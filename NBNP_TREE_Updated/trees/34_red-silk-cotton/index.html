Red silk cotton

முள் இலவம்

Bombax L.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameBombax L.
FamilyBombacaceae
Common NameRed silk cotton
Tamil Nameமுள் இலவம்
01Flowering SeasonJanuary to March/April
02Fruiting SeasonApril to June
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • A decoction of the bark is used to combat fever.
  • A decoction of the heartwood is recommended for diabetics
  • The tree is known as a "silk-cotton tree" due to the fiber produced by the seed pods, which is used to fill pillows and mattresses.
  • Some products use extracts from the plant for skincare.
  • The young leaves, petioles, and seed cake are used as fodder for cattle.
