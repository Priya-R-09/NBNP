Mulipolavu

வெண்ணாந்தை,முலிபொலவு

Pterospermum reticulatum Wight & Arn.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NamePterospermum reticulatum Wight & Arn.
FamilyMalvaceae
Common NameMulipolavu
Tamil Nameவெண்ணாந்தை,முலிபொலவு
01Flowering SeasonFebruary to April
02Fruiting SeasonMarch to November
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • Leaves are used in traditional medicine to help manage diabetes.
  • The bark and leaves are used in traditional medicine to treat diarrhea, dysentery, skin infections, and ulcers.
  • It is sometimes planted as a roadside tree.
  • The wood is used locally for making furniture, boxes, and other wooden products.
