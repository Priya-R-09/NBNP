Jamun

நாவல்

Syzygium cumini (L.) Skeels

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameSyzygium cumini (L.) Skeels
FamilyMyrtaceae
Common NameJamun
Tamil Nameநாவல்
01Flowering SeasonMarch to April
02Fruiting SeasonJune to July
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • Powdered seeds mixed with sugar and taken orally to help control diabetes.
  • Fruit juice Used to treat gastric complaints and diabetes.
  • A paste of young leaves with goat milk is used for indigestion.
  • Taking the bark decoction orally is said to relieve headaches
