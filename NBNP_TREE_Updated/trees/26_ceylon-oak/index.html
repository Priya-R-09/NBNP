Ceylon oak

பூவம்

Schleichera oleosa (Lour.) Oken

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameSchleichera oleosa (Lour.) Oken
FamilySapindoideae
Common NameCeylon oak
Tamil Nameபூவம்
01Flowering SeasonJanuary to April
02Fruiting SeasonFebruary to August
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • The oil is used in hairdressing and to promote hair growth.
  • It is also applied topically to treat acne, burns, and other skin problems.
  • As a massage oil, it is used to relieve the pain associated with rheumatism
  • That powdered seeds are applied to ulcers and wounds in cattle to remove maggots.
