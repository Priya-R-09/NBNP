Common Indian Linden

தடசு

Grewia tiliifolia A.Rich.

BOTANICAL IDENTITY
Botanical NameGrewia tiliifolia A.Rich.
FamilyMalvaceae
Common NameCommon Indian Linden
Tamil Nameதடசு
01Flowering SeasonFebruary to June
02Fruiting SeasonJuly-September
03Medicinal ValueWhole plant
02

English Information

  • Used for ulcers, skin diseases, and bleeding wounds. bark/fruit juice applied externally.
  • Wood is Used for tool handles.
  • Ripe fruits are consumed for nutrition.
  • Environmental: Helps stabilize sand dunes and is used in wasteland rehabilitation.
