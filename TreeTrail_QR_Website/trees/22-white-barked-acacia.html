White-Barked Acacia

வெள்வேல்
Acacia leucophloea (Roxb.) Willd.
FamilyFabaceae
FloweringAugust to October
FruitingApril to June
Medicinal partWhole plant
📱 This page is designed to be the destination for the QR code placed beside this tree.

Traditional & practical information

English

  • Used to treat dry coughs, diarrhea, vomiting bronchitis, and bronchial inflammation.
  • Employed to treat skin ailments like leprosy and leukoderma .
  • Employed to treat snake bites, ulcers, and bleeding gums.menstrual problems.
