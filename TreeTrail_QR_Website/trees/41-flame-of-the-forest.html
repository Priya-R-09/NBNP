TREE PROFILE · 41

Flame of the Forest

பொரசு
Butea monosperma (Lam.) Kuntze
FamilyFabaceae
FloweringJanuary to March
FruitingMay to June
Medicinal partWhole plant
📱 This page is designed to be the destination for the QR code placed beside this tree.

Traditional & practical information

English

  • Used to help manage blood glucose levels.
  • It provides habitat and food for various wildlife, such as birds, bees, and butterflies, supporting local ecosystems.
  • The vibrant flowers produce a natural dye used for coloring fabrics like silk and cotton, especially for traditional textiles.
  • The tree is revered in many Asian cultures, is associated with Hindu festivals, and is considered sacred.
