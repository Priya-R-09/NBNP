Indian Plum

இலந்தை
Ziziphus mauritiana Lam.
FamilyRhamnaceae
FloweringSeptember to November
FruitingOctober/November,March/April
Medicinal partWhole plant
📱 This page is designed to be the destination for the QR code placed beside this tree.

Traditional & practical information

English

  • Eaten fresh or dried; used in teas, juices, jams, chutneys, and pickles.
  • Rich in Vitamin C and antioxidants, it supports immune function and fights oxidative stress.
  • Leaves are a nutritious fodder for livestock and silkworms.
