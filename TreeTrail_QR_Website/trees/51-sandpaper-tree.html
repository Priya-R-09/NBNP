Sandpaper Tree

பராய்
Streblus asper Lour.
FamilyMoraceae
FloweringJanuary to March and again from July to September
FruitingApril to June.
Medicinal partWhole plant
📱 This page is designed to be the destination for the QR code placed beside this tree.

Traditional & practical information

English

  • Bark decoctions treat fever, diarrhea, and dysentery.
  • Root applications for snakebites, and liver support for jaundice.
  • Animal feed.
  • Bio-insecticide.
  • Fuel and papermaking.
