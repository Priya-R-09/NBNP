Lebbek tree

வாகை மரம்
Albizia lebbeck (L.) Benth.
FamilyMimosaceae
FloweringApril to June or September.
FruitingSeptember–October
Medicinal partWhole plant
📱 This page is designed to be the destination for the QR code placed beside this tree.

Traditional & practical information

English

  • Albizia lebbeck is used in traditional medicine for a variety of purposes, including treating respiratory issues like asthma and coughs, skin diseases, allergic reactions, and snakebites.
  • The tree is a source of timber in India and Pakistan.
