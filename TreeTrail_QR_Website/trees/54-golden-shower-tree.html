Golden Shower Tree

சரக்கொன்றை
Cassia fistula L.
FamilyFabaceae
FloweringApril to June.
FruitingDecember to April
Medicinal partWhole plant
📱 This page is designed to be the destination for the QR code placed beside this tree.

Traditional & practical information

English

  • Used in herbal jams (Avaleha) for coughs and breathing difficulties.
  • Used for purifying the blood and supporting vascular health.
  • Flower extracts are used in anti-aging and skin-whitening cosmetic products.
  • Leaves are used to treat ringworm, coughs, and snakebites; also used in wound dressings.
  • Root powder treats skin diseases; root juice has diuretic and purgative effects
