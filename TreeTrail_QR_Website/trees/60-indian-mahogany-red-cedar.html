Indian mahogany, Red cedar

சந்தன வேம்பு
Toona M.Roem. (Toona ciliata )
FamilyMeliaceae
FloweringFebruary to May
FruitingFebruary and May
Medicinal partWhole plant
📱 This page is designed to be the destination for the QR code placed beside this tree.

Traditional & practical information

English

  • The tree is highly valued for high-quality furniture, interior decoration, paneling, and cabinetry.
  • The tree Used for instruments like sitars, rudra veenas, and drums, and as a substitute for mahogany in guitars.
  • The tree planted as an ornamental, roadside tree, for erosion control, and in agroforestry
