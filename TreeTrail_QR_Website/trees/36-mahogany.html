mahogany

மஹோகனி
Swietenia mahagoni (L.) Jacq.
FamilyMeliaceae
FloweringApril – June
FruitingOctober - January
Medicinal partWhole plant
📱 This page is designed to be the destination for the QR code placed beside this tree.

Traditional & practical information

English

  • The wood's attractive appearance, durability, and ease of finishing make it ideal for high-class furniture, construction of boats,cabinets, and decorative trim.
  • The wood is used for musical instruments like guitars because of its warm tone.
  • Traditional uses include treating malaria, coughs, and tuberculosis, as well as its use as a tonic and for general wellness.
