Wild Almond Tree

பீநாறி
Sterculia foetida L.
FamilyMalvaceae
FloweringFebruary to May
FruitingMarch to May
Medicinal partWhole plant
📱 This page is designed to be the destination for the QR code placed beside this tree.

Traditional & practical information

English

  • The seeds are a good source of protein and oil, and are roasted and eaten in some regions.
  • The wood is soft but durable for interior work, and is used to make items like dugout canoes, guitars, and carved toys.
  • Powdered seeds are used as a red pigment.
