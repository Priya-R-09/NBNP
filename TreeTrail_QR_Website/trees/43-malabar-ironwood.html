Malabar Ironwood

இருபகம்
Hopea parviflora Bedd.
FamilyDipterocarpaceae
FloweringJanuary to March
FruitingJanuary and June
Medicinal partWhole parts
📱 This page is designed to be the destination for the QR code placed beside this tree.

Traditional & practical information

English

  • It has been used for railway sleepers.
  • The heartwood is a good-quality, durable wood used for construction, furniture, and, historically, plywood.
  • Decoctions from the bark are used to relieve joint pain.
